Our crop management services

Crop Planning & Production Strategy

Developing production plans based on crop type, variety, growing system, production cycle, and target yield.

Plant Growth Management

Monitoring plant development and adjusting cultivation practices to maintain balanced vegetative and reproductive growth.

Irrigation & Fertigation Management

Managing irrigation frequency, water application, fertigation, EC, and pH according to crop requirements and environmental conditions.

Plant Nutrition Management

Monitoring crop nutritional requirements and adjusting feeding programs throughout different growth stages.

Environmental Management

Optimizing temperature, humidity, ventilation, light, and other environmental conditions affecting crop development.

Pest & Disease Monitoring

Regular crop scouting and early identification of potential pest and disease problems, with integrated management strategies when required.

Crop Training & Canopy Management

Technical guidance on pruning, training, trellising, leaf removal, and canopy management to maintain proper plant balance and light penetration.

Crop Performance Monitoring

Tracking key production indicators and crop responses to identify problems early and continuously improve crop performance.
